## 2. Competitive Hierarchy & Stage Structure

1. **Vorrunde (Preliminary Stage)**: 8 regional groups of 7 teams (56 teams total). Top 3 advance to Hauptrunde; lower 4 enter Relegation.
2. **Hauptrunde (Main Stage)**: 4 groups of 6 teams (24 teams total). All teams advance to the National Playoff Bracket.
3. **Relegationsrunde**: 4 groups of 8 teams (32 teams total) fighting for qualification preservation.
4. **National Playoffs**: Round of 1/16 $\to$ Round of 1/8 $\to$ Quarterfinals $\to$ TOP4 Semifinals and National Championship.
