# Evidence-First Player Intelligence Specification
## Rheinland Falcons Basketball — JBBL / NBBL Longitudinal Intelligence Platform

---

## 1. Executive Philosophy & Epistemic Framework

The **Player Intelligence Layer** is engineered to provide the coaching staff and academy leadership with an evidence-grounded, longitudinal, and context-aware understanding of individual player performance and development.

In youth basketball development (U16 JBBL / U19 NBBL), statistical evaluation is fraught with noise, small sample sizes, extreme minute disparities, and wide biological age variations. Presenting isolated boxscore averages (e.g. *"Player averages 15 PPG"*) without context promotes flawed decision-making.

### The Five Epistemic Tiers
Every insight generated by this layer is strictly tagged with its evidentiary level:

1. **`OBSERVATION` (Descriptive Ground Truth)**: Directly observed and mathematically verifiable numbers from official match records (e.g. *"The player attempted 47 three-pointers across 19 games and made 23"*).
2. **`CONTEXT` (Normative Population Benchmark)**: Positioning the observation relative to a verified, normalized reference group (e.g. *"The player's 48.9% 3P places him in the 94th percentile among JBBL players with $\ge 100$ minutes"*).
3. **`INTERPRETATION` (Statistical Characterization)**: Evidence-bounded statistical synthesis (e.g. *"Combines elite perimeter conversion with moderate volume of 2.5 attempts per game"*).
4. **`HYPOTHESIS` (Video/Tactical Pointer)**: A structured question requiring game film or tactical verification, never presented as a proven fact (e.g. *"High conversion may be driven by assisted catch-and-shoot looks generated by team ball movement"*).
5. **`CLAIM` (Unproven / Prohibited)**: Unsupported declarations (e.g. *"This player is an NBA prospect"* or *"He is the best shooter in Germany"*) are **strictly prohibited** by the architecture.

## 3. Detailed Profile Components

### Component 1: Identity & Biological Demographics
- **`canonical_name`**: Verified full legal name.
- **`birth_date` & Chronological Age**: Derived exact age on matchday (e.g. Maximilian Becker born `2010-09-21` $\implies$ 14.1 years old during 2024/25 U16 season).
- **`height_cm`**: Official standing height (e.g. 202 cm for Fall, 175 cm for Weber).
- **`nationality`**: Primary sporting nationality.
- **`team_id` & `season_id`**: Affiliation tracking (`TEM_DEMO_U16`, `SEA_2025`).

### Component 2: Exposure, Availability & Workload
- **`games_played` (GP)** & **`games_started` (GS)**: Exposure count.
- **`minutes` & `mpg`**: Total minutes played and minutes per game.
- **`minutes_share`**: Percentage of team's total available regulation player-minutes ($200	ext{ min/game}$).
- **`dnp_count`**: Did Not Play (Coach's decision vs injury).
- **`role_proxy`**: Classification based on minutes and usage (e.g. *Primary Core Starter*, *Rotation Contributor*, *Developmental Reserve*).

### Component 3: Standard Boxscore Production
- **Scoring**: Total Points, PPG.
- **Rebounding**: Offensive Rebounds (ORB), Defensive Rebounds (DRB), Total Rebounds (TRB), RPG.
- **Playmaking & Passing**: Assists (AST), APG.
- **Defensive Counting Stats**: Steals (STL), Blocks (BLK), Fouls (PF).
- **Turnovers**: Total Turnovers, TOV/G.

### Component 4: Rate, Efficiency & Impact Metrics
- **`pts_per_40`, `reb_per_40`, `ast_per_40`, `stl_per_40`, `blk_per_40`, `tov_per_40`**: Production scaled to standard 40-minute regulation game.
- **True Shooting Percentage (`ts_pct`)**: Overall scoring efficiency accounting for 2PT, 3PT, and free throws.
- **Effective Field Goal Percentage (`efg_pct`)**: Field goal efficiency adjusting for the extra value of 3-point shots.
- **Assist-to-Turnover Ratio (`ast_to_tov`)**: Ball security vs playmaking balance.
- **Free Throw Rate (`ft_rate`)**: Ability to draw contact ($	ext{FTA} / 	ext{FGA}$).
- **3-Point Attempt Rate (`f3a_rate`)**: Perimeter orientation ($	ext{3PA} / 	ext{FGA}$).

### Component 5: Spatial Shot Diet & Creation Context
- **Shot Volume**: Total 2PA, 2PM, 2P%, Total 3PA, 3PM, 3P%, Total FTA, FTM, FT%.
- **Zone Frequency**: Rim Frequency ($\le 1.5$m), Paint Frequency (non-RA), Mid-Range Frequency, Corner 3 Frequency, Above-the-Break 3 Frequency.
- **Shot Creation**: % of Made Field Goals Assisted vs % Unassisted (self-created off the dribble).
- **Expected Points per Shot (xPTS)**: Shot quality metric based on league-wide average conversion by zone.

### Component 6: Normative League Benchmark (6-Axis Radar)
Every qualified player ($N \ge 100$ min) is benchmarked against the JBBL universe across 6 fundamental basketball dimensions:
1. **Scoring Volume**: `pts_per_40` percentile.
2. **Scoring Efficiency**: `ts_pct` percentile.
3. **Rebounding Power**: `reb_per_40` percentile.
4. **Playmaking Creation**: `ast_per_40` percentile.
5. **Ball Security**: `ast_to_tov` / Inverse `tov_per_40` percentile.
6. **Defensive Disruption**: Combined `stl_per_40` + `blk_per_40` percentile.

### Component 7: Evidence Reliability & Sample-Size Badging
Every metric group receives an explicit evidence badge based on the volume of observations:
- **`ESTABLISHED_SIGNAL`** (High confidence: e.g. $\ge 150$ total field goal attempts for FG%).
- **`USABLE_SIGNAL`** (Moderate confidence: e.g. $50	ext{--}149$ attempts).
- **`EMERGING_SIGNAL`** (Exploratory: e.g. $20	ext{--}49$ attempts).
- **`DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY`** (Small sample: e.g. $< 20$ attempts; cannot infer skill).

### Component 8: Longitudinal Trajectory & Rolling Dynamics
- **Rolling 4-Game Window**: Moving averages for PPG, TS%, RPG, APG.
- **Delta vs Season Baseline**: $\Delta = 	ext{Rolling\_4\_Metric} - 	ext{Season\_Mean\_Metric}$.
- **Trend Classification**: Categorized strictly as `POSITIVE_TREND`, `NEGATIVE_TREND`, or `STABLE` with explicit sample caveats.
- **Game-to-Game Consistency**: Standard deviation and coefficient of variation ($CV$) across appearances.

### Component 9: Structured Tactical Hypotheses
- Callouts for video analysis (e.g. *"Investigate Maximilian Becker's defensive positioning against pick-and-pop bigs"*).
- Direct linking between statistical anomalies and match video timestamps.

## 4. Grounded Prospect Analytics (Ferran Module)

### What is Grounded & Usable Today:
1. **Biological Age vs Competition Level**: Identifying players performing well while being 1–2 years younger than competition median (e.g. Maximilian Becker at 14.1 yrs in U16).
2. **Per-40 Scaling for Low-Minute High-Impact Prospects**: Evaluating per-minute dominance for rotation players before expanding their role.
3. **Efficiency vs Usage Scatter**: Mapping players across the volume/efficiency frontier.
4. **Rim Dominance & Defensive Disruption**: Shot blocking rates, offensive rebounding rates, and restricted area shooting percentage.

### What is Explicitly Excluded (Anti-Fabrication Policy):
- Synthetic "Prospect Potential Scores" (0–100 pseudo-ratings).
- Speculative NBA / Euroleague draft projections.
- Inferred positions when unlisted in match sheets.
