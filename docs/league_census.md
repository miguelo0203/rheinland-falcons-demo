# Complete League Census — JBBL Universe & Historical Match Inventories

## 1. League Competition Architecture

The Jugend-Basketball-Bundesliga (JBBL U16) is Germany's top-tier national championship for male athletes under 16 years of age.
It features **56 participating clubs** nationwide divided into geographic Vorrunde groups, followed by competitive bifurcation into **Hauptrunde** (top seeds advancing to national playoffs) and **Relegationsrunde** (lower seeds fighting for qualification retention).
